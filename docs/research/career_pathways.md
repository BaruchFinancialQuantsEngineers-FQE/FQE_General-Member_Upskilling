# Career Pathways into Quantitative Finance

Prepared for FQE (Financial Quants and Engineers, Baruch College). Research date: October 1, 2026.

## How this document was verified

Every factual claim carries a source URL. Direct page fetches were blocked by the research environment's network proxy for every firm domain tried (citadel.com, imc.com, janestreet.com, mfe.baruch.cuny.edu, sig.com, optiver.com). All claims below were therefore verified from search-engine result content returned for those URLs, restricted to the firm's own domain wherever possible. The "Verification status" table at the end separates three levels:

- **Verified (primary):** the claim appeared in search results drawn from the firm's, program's, or competition's own domain.
- **Secondary only:** the claim appeared only on a third-party site (blog, career-guide site, university events calendar). Treat as likely but unconfirmed.
- **Unverified:** no source located; stated as inference.

Members should open each link before acting on a date. Firm pages change during the season.

## 1. Role families

### Summary table

| Role | What the work is | Typical interview content | FQE plan component that maps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quantitative trader | Identify market signals, build and run trading strategies, price risk, make markets | Mental math, probability, expected value, market-making games, brainteasers | Trading Competition, market-making drill, Brainteaser Battle, Math Competition |
| Quantitative researcher | Build predictive models from data, test hypotheses, turn research into strategies | Probability and statistics, coding, data analysis, research discussion | Math Competition, pandas case study, backtesting Hackathon |
| Quantitative developer / software engineer | Build trading systems, research infrastructure, low-latency execution | Coding (algorithms, data structures), systems design, some probability | Hackathon (Python package and tests), Git setup, SQL |
| Bank strats / quantitative analytics / risk | Pricing and risk models, derivatives modeling, model risk, trading automation | Probability, stochastic calculus for some desks, coding, finance basics | Math Competition, SQL, pandas case study |
| Asset manager research and data roles | Clean data, build factor and risk models, analyze backtests | Statistics, Python, finance knowledge | SQL, pandas case study, Hackathon |

### Quantitative trader

Jane Street describes its Quantitative Trader internship as pairing interns with traders "who teach how to identify market signals, analyze and execute strategies, construct quantitative models, conduct statistical analysis, and build trading intuition," reinforced by "team-based mock trading sessions." The listed base salary is $300,000 annualized. No specific major or programming language is required. ([Jane Street QT Internship, New York](https://www.janestreet.com/join-jane-street/position/8617344002/))

Interview content at Jane Street: phone interviews, then final rounds that "may include problem solving, probability and statistics, coding (in any language of your choice), data analysis, and general interests." The firm states that interviews do not require prior finance knowledge or advanced college math. ([Jane Street Trading Interviews](https://www.janestreet.com/trading-interviews/)) Jane Street publishes a free "Probability & Markets" primer. ([Jane Street trading-interview PDF](https://www.janestreet.com/static/pdfs/trading-interview.pdf))

Optiver's first application step for trading roles includes an eight-minute mental-math numerical test with no calculator, and assessments can only be taken once every eight months. ([Optiver Numerical Test](https://numericaltest.optiver.com/); [Optiver Europe recruitment FAQ](https://www.optiver.com/join-us/stories/optiver-europe-recruitment-faq/))

Susquehanna (SIG) trains traders through game theory and decision science, including poker, to teach "decision making under uncertainty" and expected value of a trade. ([SIG Game Theory + Decision Science](https://sig.com/who-we-are/game-theory-decision-science/)) SIG's Quantitative Trader Internship splits the day between a trading desk and classroom training in options theory and decision science. ([SIG QT Internship Summer 2027, New York](https://careers.sig.com/quantitative-trading-internships-co-ops/jobs/10718?lang=en-us))

**FQE mapping.** The market-making drill and Trading Competition rehearse quoting and settlement under uncertainty. The Brainteaser Battle rehearses the probability and expected-value questions. Mental-math speed is tested directly by Optiver and is not yet a separate FQE drill.

### Quantitative researcher

Two Sigma describes Quantitative Research as using "the scientific method to develop sophisticated and predictive investment models," with interns applying "quantitative techniques to look for patterns in the world's data." Weekly base pay for its intern role is listed at $4,900 (Bachelors), $5,000 (Masters), and $5,500 (PhD). ([Two Sigma QR Intern, Summer 2027, New York](https://careers.twosigma.com/careers/JobDetail/New-York-New-York-United-States-Quantitative-Researcher-Intern-2027-Summer/13945))

Hudson River Trading (HRT) Algorithm Development interns research and implement automated trading strategies, rotate among high-frequency, mid-frequency, and machine-learning teams, and work in Python and C++. The Summer 2027 program is offered in NYC, London, and Singapore. ([HRT Algorithm Development Internship, Summer 2027](https://www.hudsonrivertrading.com/hrt-job/algorithm-development-quant-research-trading-internship-summer-2027/))

D. E. Shaw's Quantitative Analyst intern works on "a research project that typically involves exploring a variety of statistical modeling techniques and writing software to analyze financial data." The 12-week New York program runs June to August 2027 with a listed monthly base salary of $25,000. ([D. E. Shaw QA Intern, New York, Summer 2027](https://www.deshaw.com/careers/quantitative-analyst-intern-new-york-summer-2027-5890)) D. E. Shaw publishes an interviewing guide. ([D. E. Shaw interviewing guide PDF](https://www.deshaw.com/assets/articles/D_E_Shaw_Group_Interviewing_Guide.pdf))

**FQE mapping.** The pandas case study (returns, rolling volatility, groupby) and the backtesting Hackathon (look-ahead control, Sharpe, drawdown) mirror the "acquire data, build model, test strategy" loop these firms describe. The Math Competition covers the probability and statistics base.

### Quantitative developer and software engineer

HRT lists a Summer 2027 Software Engineering Internship (C++ or Python). ([HRT SWE Internship, Summer 2027](https://www.hudsonrivertrading.com/hrt-job/software-engineering-internship-c-or-python-summer-2027/)) Citadel Securities' Software Engineer intern roles in Miami and New York ask for "exceptional programming and design skills" and "familiarity with probability and statistics." ([Citadel Securities SWE Intern, US](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/details/software-engineer-intern-us/)) Jane Street publishes a software engineering interview preparation page. ([Jane Street SWE interview prep](https://www.janestreet.com/preparing-for-a-software-engineering-interview/))

**FQE mapping.** The Hackathon's tested Python package, Git workflow, and SQL exercises are the closest FQE analogues. FQE does not currently include data-structures or algorithms practice.

### Bank strats, quantitative analytics, and risk

Goldman Sachs quantitative strategists "work on alpha generating strategies, discuss portfolio allocation problems, and build models for prediction, pricing, trading automation, data analysis." Knowledge of stochastic calculus and financial models is "an added advantage." The Engineering and Quantitative Strategists summer program is a ten-week internship for graduate students. ([Goldman Sachs Careers in Engineering](https://www.goldmansachs.com/careers/our-firm/engineering); [GS 2027 Summer Associate Program, Americas](https://www.goldmansachs.com/careers/students/programs-and-internships/americas/summer-associate-program))

J.P. Morgan's Quantitative Analytics Summer Associate program teaches "financial engineering, derivatives modeling, risk management, and machine learning." ([J.P. Morgan Quantitative Finance program](https://careers.jpmorgan.com/careers/programs/qr-summer-associate))

Morgan Stanley's Strats and Modeling group creates "mathematical models, software tools and libraries" for securities trading. Its North America Quantitative Finance Summer Analyst page showed no open opportunities at the time of search. ([Morgan Stanley Quantitative Finance Summer Analyst](https://www.morganstanley.com/people-opportunities/students-graduates/programs/institutional-securities/quantitative-finance/summer-analyst-north-america))

**FQE mapping.** Banks place more weight on the Math Competition topics (distributions, linear algebra, series) and on SQL. Note that the J.P. Morgan and Goldman programs found are summer associate (graduate) programs; these are the natural target for Baruch MFE students.

### Asset manager research, risk, and data roles

AQR lists 2027 Research, Risk, and Portfolio Implementation summer analyst roles in Greenwich, CT. Research work includes "acquiring and cleaning data, building models, analyzing backtested trading strategies," and Python is preferred. ([AQR 2027 Research Summer Analyst](https://careers.aqr.com/jobs/open-positions/greenwich-ct/2027-research-summer-analyst/7895583?gh_jid=7895583); [AQR 2027 Risk Summer Analyst](https://careers.aqr.com/jobs/open-positions/greenwich-ct/2027-risk-summer-analyst/7926692?gh_jid=7926692))

BlackRock's Analytics and Modeling function "researches, develops and implements innovative quantitative models used to assess financial risk" through Aladdin. BlackRock also lists a 2027 Quantitative Masters Internship in New York. ([BlackRock Analytics and Modeling](https://careers.blackrock.com/students-and-graduates-functions-analytics-modeling); [BlackRock 2027 Quantitative Masters Internship](https://careers.blackrock.com/job/new-york/2027-quantitative-masters-internship-program-technology-analytics-and-modeling-new-york/45831/100661849952))

**FQE mapping.** AQR's description of research work reads almost like the FQE Hackathon brief. The pandas case study and SQL exercises map directly to data-cleaning work.

## Implications for the FQE plan

1. **Treat Summer 2027 as an active cycle now.** Quant postings at Jane Street, HRT, SIG, Two Sigma, and D. E. Shaw are live. Add an "apply this month" list to Checkpoint 1 (October 14) rather than waiting for Checkpoint 3.
2. **Push first- and second-year members to discovery programs.** The SIG New York Discovery Program closes November 16, 2026, and leads to Summer 2028 internships. Jane Street INSIGHT runs January 4 to 9, 2027. Add both to the application tracker template.
3. **Send a team to the Cornell Trading Competition next year and track Rotman.** Cornell is in NYC and open to undergraduate and master's students with no experience required. The 2026 deadline (September 20) has passed, so note it for fall 2027. Rotman runs February 18 to 20, 2027.
4. **Add a mental-math speed drill to the market-making folder.** Optiver's first trading screen is an eight-minute, calculator-free numerical test. The current FQE plan has no timed arithmetic practice.
5. **Point members to the free primary prep materials.** Jane Street's probability primer and monthly puzzles fit the Brainteaser Battle and Math Competition, and they do not conflict with the plan's rule against reproducing book problems.
6. **Frame the Hackathon around the AQR and HRT research loop.** Their job pages describe acquiring data, building models, and analyzing backtests. Ask Hackathon teams to write a README that reads like that loop; it becomes a résumé line.
7. **Prepare for IMC Prosperity and Citadel datathons in spring.** Prosperity 2026 ran April 14 to 30 with tutorial access from mid-March. A spring FQE desk could form Prosperity teams in February using the Hackathon package.
8. **Advertise the Pre-MFE seminars to juniors, seniors, and MFE applicants.** The Fall 2026 Probability seminar starts October 19, 2026. More than 20% of current Baruch MFE students completed Pre-MFE seminars. Also confirm the FQE Trading Competition date (October 2 on the FQE site versus October 9 in the plan).
